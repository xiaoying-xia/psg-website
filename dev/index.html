Who We Are

Two continents, one team

PSG Group LLC (More Construction, LLC) helps Chinese and Southeast-Asian manufacturers plan, build, and commission facilities in the United States — a local American delivery team working hand-in-hand with our engineering teams overseas.

With 25+ years and 100+ facilities delivered worldwide for global manufacturers, PSG has operated in the U.S. since 2019. Clients get local execution plus the full technical depth of our overseas companies — rapid response across time zones and zero-barrier communication with headquarters.
