Who We Are

A proven industrial builder

PSG Group LLC (More Construction, LLC) provides engineering, technical advisory, design review, mechanical systems coordination, and commissioning support for industrial and mission-critical projects.

With more than 25 years of experience and a track record of 100+ facilities delivered worldwide, PSG originated in Asia and has operated in the United States since 2019 — serving both local and international clients with precision and technical expertise.
